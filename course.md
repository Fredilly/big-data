# Proposed Teaching Portfolio | March–July 2027

**Data Science and Big Data Technology · Chengdu · English-medium instruction**

These are **proposed** syllabi for discussion with the university, not previously delivered university courses. One teaching hour is 45 minutes. Course allocations and university-approved textbooks, assessment rules and learning prerequisites require confirmation. The four 64-hour courses each span **16 teaching hours per week over four weeks**, within the published 20-hour weekly limit if taught separately. The 4-hour introduction is a short module.

| Course | Teaching hours | Delivery plan |
|---|---:|---|
| [Exploration in Data Science](#exploration-in-data-science) | 64 | 4 weeks × 16 teaching hours |
| [Database and Information Management](#database-and-information-management) | 64 | 4 weeks × 16 teaching hours |
| [Object-oriented Programming (Java)](#objectoriented-programming-java) | 64 | 4 weeks × 16 teaching hours |
| [Organizational Website and Database Management](#organizational-website-and-database-management) | 64 | 4 weeks × 16 teaching hours |
| [Introduction to Programming and Machine Intelligence](#introduction-to-programming-and-machine-intelligence) | 4 | 4-hour session |

**Scheduling note:** All listed courses total 260 teaching hours. At no more than 20 hours weekly, one lecturer needs at least 13 teaching weeks to deliver all five. The university must specify which courses are assigned and how the four/five-week placement is scheduled.

## Exploration in Data Science

**Core content:** Python and notebooks; data collection and cleaning; SQL and Pandas; exploratory statistics; visualization; introductory machine learning; reproducibility and ethics.

## Introduction to Programming and Machine Intelligence

**4 teaching hours · Short introduction**

**Learning outcomes:** Complete a short guided coding exercise and explain one failure mode of an AI system.

**Core content:** Algorithms and Python basics; model inputs/outputs; a guided classifier or rule-based demo; AI strengths, limits and responsible use.

| Teaching block | Hours | Learning activities |
|---|---:|---|
| Unit 1 | 1 | Algorithmic thinking and simple variables |
| Unit 2 | 1 | Guided programming exercise |
| Unit 3 | 1 | Demonstration of a basic AI model |
| Unit 4 | 1 | Responsible use and reflection |

**Assessment proposal:** Participation and a short formative exercise; no assumed formal grade.

**Example capstone or exercise:** Complete a short guided coding exercise and explain one failure mode of an AI system.


## Delivery and preparation

- Slides, instructor notes, starter code, graded lab exercises, rubrics and reproducible examples will be prepared before assigned teaching begins.
- Teaching will explicitly distinguish demonstrated professional experience from areas under active instructional preparation. **Java OOP is a preparation priority**, with a tested sample project and lesson demonstration to be completed before accepting its assignment.
- Materials can be adapted to the university's required Java version, database platform, curriculum outcomes, and assessment policy.
